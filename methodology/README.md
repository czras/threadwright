# Threadwright Methodology

This directory contains the intellectual and methodological body of Threadwright.

## Foundations & Provenance

[`foundations.md`](foundations.md) explains the three distinct conceptual layers and their evolutionary relationship:

1. **Creator's Lens** — recurring preferences and abstract character shaping the work
2. **Homo Sapiens Agenticus (HSA)** — thesis on human–agent cognitive partnership
3. **Threadwright** — synthesis of the two into a practical methodology

The relationship is evolutionary with feedback: `Reality → Lens → HSA → Threadwright → Practice → Observation → Refinement ↺`

Provenance is separated from prescription — practitioners need not share the creator's lens or adopt HSA.

## Intellectual foundation

[`homo-sapiens-agenticus.md`](homo-sapiens-agenticus.md) presents the **Homo Sapiens Agenticus** thesis. It is intentionally independent of Threadwright and uses its own conceptual vocabulary. It concerns human–agent cognitive partnership and the complementary capabilities of humans and persistent agents.

See [`foundations.md`](foundations.md) for the full provenance and relationship to Threadwright.

## Threadwright

[`threadwright.md`](threadwright.md) defines Threadwright as an infrastructure concept and methodology for the evolution of intent and knowledge.

Threadwright is not identical to the HSA thesis. HSA is the broader thesis; Threadwright is a potential technological response to compatible problems and opportunities identified by that thesis.

See [`foundations.md`](foundations.md) for the full provenance and relationship to the creator's lens.

[`principles.md`](principles.md) states the cross-cutting constraints that guide how Threadwright is applied, interpreted, and evolved when the methodology itself does not prescribe a specific answer. Principles are general; the five invariants in [`threadwright.md`](threadwright.md#8-five-invariants) are the structural rules they inform, and heuristics/activities are how they are applied in practice.

## Operational knowledge

- [`heuristics/`](heuristics/) — independently addressable guidance for applying the methodology.
- [`scenarios/`](scenarios/) — independently addressable situations that demonstrate, challenge, or validate the methodology.
- [`activities/`](activities/) — reusable definitions of structured procedures (e.g. reviewing a methodology change). Distinct from the core **Activity** concept in [`threadwright.md`](threadwright.md#2-core-model), which is an instance of work performed; a file under `activities/` is a reusable definition of *how* such work can be carried out.
- [`artifacts/`](artifacts/) — persistent artifacts produced while developing and applying the methodology.

The conceptual relationship is:

```text
Creator's Lens
       ↓
Homo Sapiens Agenticus (thesis)
       ↓
Threadwright (synthesis → infrastructure → methodology)
       ↓
heuristics + scenarios + activities
       ↓
potential tooling
```

The arrows indicate a relationship of ideas and evolutionary feedback, not a requirement that every Threadwright use case adopt the HSA thesis or any particular tooling.
