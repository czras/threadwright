# Threadwright Methodology

This directory contains the intellectual and methodological body of Threadwright.

## Intellectual foundation

[`homo-sapiens-agenticus.md`](homo-sapiens-agenticus.md) presents the **Homo Sapiens Agenticus** thesis. It is intentionally independent of Threadwright and uses its own conceptual vocabulary. It concerns human–agent cognitive partnership and the complementary capabilities of humans and persistent agents.

## Threadwright

[`threadwright.md`](threadwright.md) defines Threadwright as an infrastructure concept and methodology for the evolution of intent and knowledge.

Threadwright is not identical to the HSA thesis. HSA is the broader thesis; Threadwright is a potential technological response to compatible problems and opportunities identified by that thesis.

## Operational knowledge

- [`heuristics/`](heuristics/) — independently addressable guidance for applying the methodology.
- [`scenarios/`](scenarios/) — independently addressable situations that demonstrate, challenge, or validate the methodology.
- [`artifacts/`](artifacts/) — persistent artifacts produced while developing and applying the methodology.

The conceptual relationship is:

```text
Homo Sapiens Agenticus
        ↓
       thesis
        ↓
   Threadwright
        ↓
 infrastructure concept
        ↓
    methodology
        ↓
 heuristics + scenarios
        ↓
 potential tooling
```

The arrows indicate a relationship of ideas and implementation, not a requirement that every Threadwright use case adopt the HSA thesis or any particular tooling.
