# Scenario — SSO / Enterprise Authentication

## Situation

A product needs enterprise SSO. Customers want broad identity-provider support, security has requirements, engineering has architectural constraints, and sales has commercial commitments.

A possible Change is:

> Enable target enterprise customers to authenticate using supported enterprise identity providers while satisfying applicable security and product constraints.

## Application

Relevant artifacts may include customer requirements, security requirements, constraints, architecture proposals, threat-model findings, implementation plans, decisions, and test or deployment evidence.

If engineering proposes architecture A, security raises a concern, and sales considers the supported scope commercially unacceptable, the conflict becomes a consequential gap.

The decision becomes:

> Which architecture and supported scope should be accepted?

Investigation is performed only to the extent necessary to make that decision responsibly. The resulting decision is recorded with its basis and authority.

## Demonstrates

Competing concerns can coexist without new primitives; contradictions can be represented through artifacts and relationships; decisions do not require certainty; authority remains a human concern; implementation is not automatically outcome achievement.
