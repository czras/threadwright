# SCENARIO-0001 — Greenfield Product Idea

## Situation

A person or team has a completely new product idea. At the beginning there may be little more than: "There may be an opportunity for an AI-assisted product."

The intended Change might initially be to determine whether the opportunity is worth pursuing. Later it could become producing a prototype for personal dogfooding, creating a beta, or creating a commercially viable MVP. These are different outcomes and therefore different Changes.

## Application

The team maintains problem hypotheses, customer hypotheses, market evidence, constraints, business assumptions, proposed product concepts, decisions, experiments, and results.

```text
Opportunity hypothesis
    ↓
Identify consequential unknowns
    ↓
Determine decisions affected
    ↓
Choose useful investigation
    ↓
Capture evidence
    ↓
Reassess
```

If evidence weakens the original opportunity, the team may modify the idea, target another segment, change the intended outcome, create another Change, or abandon the opportunity.

## Demonstrates

The methodology can begin with an idea. It does not require a pre-existing product, requirements document, architecture, roadmap, or prescribed prototype/beta/MVP lifecycle.

The methodology cannot determine whether the idea is valuable; it structures how the team investigates and decides.
