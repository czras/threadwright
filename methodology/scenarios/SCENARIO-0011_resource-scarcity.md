# SCENARIO-0011 — Resource Scarcity

## Situation

A team has 20 consequential gaps, five possible activities, one engineer, and two days of available capacity.

## Application

The team considers consequence, uncertainty, ability to block progress, downstream rework, dependencies, cost, reversibility, and available capacity.

The activity chosen is the one that appears most useful in the actual context. No mandatory priority formula is required.

## Demonstrates

The methodology structures prioritization without pretending to compute the optimal answer. Prioritization remains human judgment.
