# SCENARIO-0009 — Urgent Production Change

## Situation

A production incident is causing customer impact. There is no time to construct a comprehensive specification before acting.

## Application

Create a minimal incident artifact containing observed behavior, affected systems, known impact, current hypothesis, mitigation decision, and result.

```text
Observed incident
    ↓
Consequential gap
    ↓
Hypothesis / decision
    ↓
Mitigation activity
    ↓
Observed result
    ↓
Reassess
```

The team may act under uncertainty. The methodology requires uncertainty and the basis for consequential decisions to be represented appropriately; it does not require complete certainty before action.

## Demonstrates

Threadwright is not a documentation-before-action process. Persistence means meaningful work leaves relevant results in artifacts; it does not require exhaustive paperwork before urgent action.
