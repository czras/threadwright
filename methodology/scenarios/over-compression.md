# Over-Compression

**Heuristic:** H9 — Generate Broadly, Compress Deliberately

## Context

An agent generates a large amount of potentially useful material. A reviewer is asked to compress the result for compactness.

## Generation

Generate broadly, allowing:

- alternatives;
- relationships;
- explanations;
- evidence;
- contextual information.

## Compression

Aggressively remove material that appears redundant, verbose, or structurally unnecessary. Optimize for compactness.

## Observation

Over-compression may remove information that was not obviously necessary in isolation but contributes to:

- context;
- comprehension;
- traceability;
- navigation;
- discovery;
- understanding of relationships.

The resulting document may be shorter while providing less value.

## Evaluation

Compare:

- generated result;
- aggressively compressed result;
- appropriately compressed result.

Evaluate:

- comprehension;
- reader value;
- navigability;
- structural clarity;
- cognitive load;
- information loss.

## Question

> Can compactness itself become an optimization proxy that is improved at the expense of the actual objective?

This scenario directly exercises the objective/proxy distinction:

> **The objective is not to minimize the amount of material.**

The objective is to provide useful knowledge with sufficient structure and comprehension at acceptable cognitive cost. Compactness is a means of achieving that objective.

## Boundary Condition

A compact result is not necessarily a short result.

**Compactness means useful density, not minimum volume.**
