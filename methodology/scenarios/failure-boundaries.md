# Scenario — Methodology Failure vs Judgment Failure vs Organizational Failure

## Methodology failure

The methodology is violated: meaningful work is lost, implementation is represented as outcome achievement, evidence is overstated, contradictions are hidden, new evidence is ignored, or the current state is not reassessed.

## Judgment failure

The methodology is followed but humans make a bad decision: the wrong market or architecture is selected, the wrong feature is prioritized, customer needs are misunderstood, or excessive risk is accepted.

## Organizational / external failure

The methodology encounters conditions it cannot solve: no decision authority, insufficient resources, organizational conflict, missing expertise, legal ambiguity, or unexpected market conditions.

Threadwright can represent these problems and make them visible. It cannot eliminate them.
