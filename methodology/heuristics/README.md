# Application Heuristics

These heuristics describe how humans can practically apply the Threadwright methodology. They are guidance, not additional methodology primitives.

## Core Heuristics (H1–H4)

- [H1 — Address consequential gaps](address-consequential-gaps.md)
- [H2 — Frame uncertainty around decisions](frame-uncertainty-around-decisions.md)
- [H3 — Choose useful work](choose-useful-work.md)
- [H4 — Reassess](reassess.md)

## Extended Heuristics (from ASSESSMENT-0008/0009)

- [Choose Knowledge-Generating Action](choose-knowledge-action.md) — Operationalizes I5 Optimization
- [Dynamic Think/Experience Boundary](dynamic-think-experience-boundary.md) — Recognizing boundary shifts

## Cross-Cutting

- [Epistemic Integrity](epistemic-integrity.md) — Applies to all heuristics

## Applying Them Together

```text
Current state
    ↓
H1: What consequential gaps exist?
    ↓
H2: What decision or action does the uncertainty affect?
    ↓
H3: What activity would most usefully address it?
    ↓
Choose Knowledge-Generating Action (manufactured vs experiential)
    ↓
Dynamic Think/Experience Boundary check
    ↓
Perform activity
    ↓
H4: Reassess
    ↓
Evolve Intent from Messy Input (if evidence shifts understanding)
    ↓
Repeat
```

The heuristics intentionally do not provide deterministic answers. Human judgment remains central.
