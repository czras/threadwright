## Applying Them Together

```text
Current state
    ↓
HEURISTIC-0001: What consequential gaps exist?
    ↓
HEURISTIC-0002: What decision or action does the uncertainty affect?
    ↓
HEURISTIC-0003: What activity would most usefully address it?
    ↓
HEURISTIC-0005: Choose Knowledge-Generating Action (manufactured vs experiential)
    ↓
HEURISTIC-0006: Dynamic Think/Experience Boundary check
    ↓
Perform activity
    ↓
HEURISTIC-0004: Reassess
    ↓
HEURISTIC-0007: Evolve Intent from Messy Input (if evidence shifts understanding)
    ↓
Repeat
```

The heuristics intentionally do not provide deterministic answers. Human judgment remains central.
