# CHANGE — Threadwright Foundations and Provenance 🧵

## Intent

Make explicit the provenance and relationship between the creator's evolving but relatively stable professional lens, the HSA thesis, and Threadwright as their synthesis, while preserving their distinct identities and allowing all three to evolve through practice and observation.

The purpose is not to add another methodological subsystem to Threadwright, but to make its foundations and provenance explicit.

---

## Input

### The creator's lens

The creator has a particular way of engaging with reality, shaped by decades of software engineering, building, running, learning, and living.

The lens is evolving, but relatively stable. It is characterized by recurring preferences including:

- Agile
- Lean
- Minimal
- Concise
- Explicit
- Evidence-oriented
- Evolutionary
- Interconnected

At a more abstract level, these preferences reflect a recurring attraction to:

- simplicity
- explicitness
- adaptability
- economy
- interconnectedness
- learning through observation

These are characteristics of the creator's way of engaging with reality, not universal rules or principles that others are expected to adopt.

### HSA

Homo sapiens agenticus (HSA) is the creator's emerging understanding of agentic behavior and technology.

It explores what becomes possible when persistent agentic systems become a normal extension of human capabilities.

HSA is a thesis and area of exploration, not a prediction that agents should replace human judgment.

### Threadwright

Threadwright is the synthesis of the creator's lens and the HSA understanding.

It is a practical, implementation-agnostic methodology for helping humans pursue intentions and outcomes with agentic systems.

Threadwright therefore did not arise simply from HSA, nor is it merely a codification of the creator's preferences.

It emerged where the creator's existing way of working and thinking encountered the possibilities and problems introduced by agentic systems.

---

## Relationship

The relationship is evolutionary rather than linear:

**Reality → creator's lens → HSA understanding → Threadwright synthesis → practice → observation → refinement**

The three remain distinct:

- **The lens** is how the creator tends to engage with reality.
- **HSA** is the creator's emerging understanding of agentic behavior and technology.
- **Threadwright** is the synthesis of the two into a practical methodology.

Practice can provide evidence that changes the understanding of HSA, changes Threadwright, or reveals something new about the lens itself.

---

## Provenance is not prescription

The provenance of Threadwright explains why it has the character it does.

It does not mean that practitioners must share all of the creator's preferences, worldview, or conclusions.

Someone can use Threadwright without adopting the creator's entire lens.

Likewise, someone can share the HSA thesis while developing a different methodology.

Threadwright should therefore be evaluated by what it enables and by evidence from practice, rather than by agreement with its creator's personal preferences.

---

## Observational evidence

The creator's personal site provides an example of this same mode of emergence.

The current site was not designed from a fully articulated personal-brand model. It emerged from relatively raw material — a CV, running history, and other personal fragments — which were placed into a Markdown file and then explored and evolved through interaction with an agent while building the site.

This is relevant evidence for the lens itself:

> The lens can be discovered through the artifacts and choices that emerge from working, rather than only described beforehand.

This reinforces an important characteristic of the broader approach:

**observe first, name later.**

---

## Desired outcome

Threadwright has an explicit foundational layer that explains:

1. where it came from;
2. how the creator's lens relates to HSA;
3. why Threadwright is a synthesis rather than a direct implementation of either;
4. how all three can continue to evolve through practice and observation.

This layer should provide provenance without prescribing the methodology.

---

## Non-goals

- Do not turn the creator's preferences into Threadwright principles.
- Do not make HSA a prerequisite for using Threadwright.
- Do not make Threadwright a product implementation of HSA.
- Do not collapse the lens, HSA, and Threadwright into a single concept.
- Do not prematurely define the creator's lens as a complete philosophy or manifesto.
- Do not add methodological rules merely because they can be traced to the creator's preferences.
- Do not treat observations as decisions or conclusions.

---

## Acceptance criteria

- The distinction between lens, HSA, and Threadwright is explicit.
- Their provenance and relationship are understandable.
- The creator's recognizable preferences remain visible.
- The abstract character of the lens is also visible.
- The relationship is represented as evolutionary and feedback-driven.
- Provenance is clearly separated from prescription.
- Observations from practice can contribute evidence without immediately becoming doctrine.
- The foundation can evolve as Threadwright evolves.
