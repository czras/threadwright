# RESEARCH-0005: Human–Agent Collaboration and the Conceptual Territory of Threadwright

## Status

Initiated. Research not yet conducted.

**Superseedes**: RESEARCH-0004, RESEARCH-0000

## Purpose

Threadwright has so far been developed primarily from first principles, informed by the practical circumstances from which it originated but without systematically positioning its concepts within the existing academic, methodological, and technological landscape.

This research establishes that external context.

The objective is not to prove that Threadwright is novel, nor to find an existing system that can be declared equivalent to it. The objective is to understand the conceptual territory surrounding Threadwright so that its future development can be grounded in existing knowledge where appropriate and its genuinely distinctive aspects can be identified more precisely.

The investigation should also revisit previously deferred research questions where they naturally belong within this broader territory, including the possible relevance of evolutionary computation to Threadwright's mechanisms of adaptation and evolution.

## Research Questions

### Primary question

**What existing academic concepts, research, methodologies, tools, and products occupy the conceptual territory surrounding Threadwright, and what does their comparison imply for the definition and development of Threadwright?**

### Supporting questions

1. What established fields study human–AI or human–agent collaboration?
2. How do those fields conceptualize:
   - agency
   - initiative
   - responsibility
   - human judgment
   - collaboration
   - adaptation
   - context
   - decision-making
   - oversight
   - learning?
3. Which Threadwright concepts already have established equivalents or close analogues?
4. Which Threadwright concepts overlap with, duplicate, or unnecessarily rename existing concepts?
5. Which established vocabulary should Threadwright adopt to make its concepts easier to reason about and position?
6. Which existing concepts are insufficient to express something Threadwright is trying to capture?
7. What gaps exist between academic models of human–agent collaboration and practical agentic tools and methodologies?
8. What existing practical systems or methodologies implement parts of the Threadwright thesis?
9. What can existing approaches teach us about the boundaries and applicability of Threadwright?
10. Which previously proposed Threadwright concepts, heuristics, invariants, or constructs should be reconsidered in light of the neighbouring domain?
11. What previously considered research directions can be absorbed into this broader investigation rather than pursued independently?
12. In particular, what role, if any, does evolutionary computation have in the conceptual territory relevant to Threadwright's adaptation and evolution?

## Scope

The research should consider both **conceptual/academic** and **practical/technological** neighbours.

Potential areas include, but are not limited to:

### Human–Agent Collaboration

- Human–AI collaboration
- Human–AI teaming
- mixed-initiative interaction
- human–computer interaction
- hybrid intelligence
- co-intelligence / collaborative intelligence
- agency and agency allocation
- meaningful human control and oversight
- human judgment in AI-assisted decision-making

### Pursuit, Intent, and Decision-Making

- intent-driven systems
- sensemaking
- joint decision-making
- adaptive planning
- human–AI decision processes
- problem framing and exploration

### Adaptation and Evolution

- learning and feedback systems
- organizational or methodological learning
- adaptive processes
- evolutionary computation
- evolutionary approaches to optimization or process adaptation
- other relevant theories of selection, adaptation, and evolution

### Practical Agentic Methodologies and Tools

Examples may include, but are not limited to:

- Spec Kit
- BMad Method
- Nautilus
- agentic software-development methodologies
- agent orchestration and workflow systems
- other tools or products implementing relevant aspects of the human–agent relationship

The scope is intentionally open at initiation. The research should follow meaningful connections rather than assume that the listed areas are exhaustive or equally relevant.

## Intended Analysis

The research should produce a crosswalk between Threadwright and its neighbouring concepts.

For relevant external concepts, determine whether the relationship to Threadwright is:

- equivalent
- overlapping
- complementary
- a refinement
- a prerequisite
- contradictory
- unrelated
- or potentially novel

Threadwright concepts should similarly be examined for:

- existing established terminology that should replace them
- duplicate or overlapping constructs that should be consolidated
- concepts that should be retained despite existing terminology because Threadwright uses them in a materially different sense
- concepts that appear to represent a genuine gap or synthesis

The goal is **conceptual consolidation**, not accumulation.

Threadwright should not maintain bespoke terminology merely because it was invented during its own development.

## Expected Outputs

The research is expected to produce:

1. **A domain map**
   A structured overview of the relevant academic and practical territory.

2. **External references**
   Links to original research, papers, projects, methodologies, tools, and products wherever possible.

3. **A vocabulary map**
   Established terminology relevant to Threadwright and recommendations for terminology adoption.

4. **A Threadwright concept crosswalk**
   Existing Threadwright concepts compared against neighbouring concepts.

5. **Consolidation candidates**
   Threadwright concepts, heuristics, invariants, or constructs that should potentially be merged, renamed, replaced, or removed.

6. **Gap analysis**
   Areas where neighbouring fields appear not to adequately capture aspects relevant to Threadwright.

7. **Validation implications**
   Potential consequences for Threadwright's test scenarios and validation strategy, including candidate scenarios to add, modify, or remove.

8. **Research directions**
   Questions revealed by the investigation that require deeper research.

## Relationship to Existing Research

This research absorbs previously deferred investigation where that investigation falls naturally within the broader conceptual territory.

In particular, an earlier research artifact was created for investigating **evolutionary computation and its possible relevance to Threadwright**, but that research was deferred and was never conducted independently.

That investigation should therefore not be treated as completed prior work. Its unresolved research question is incorporated into RESEARCH-0005 and will be evaluated alongside other approaches to adaptation and evolution.

This preserves the earlier research artifact as part of Threadwright's development history while avoiding parallel investigations of overlapping scope.

## Relationship to Threadwright Evolution

This research is intentionally separated from subsequent modification of Threadwright.

The research should **not directly modify** Threadwright concepts, heuristics, invariants, artifacts, or validation scenarios.

Instead:

```text
External research
       ↓
Research findings
       ↓
Assessment
       ↓
Decision about Threadwright
       ↓
Change, if warranted
```

Where research findings imply changes to Threadwright, those implications should subsequently be evaluated through the appropriate Assessment and, where necessary, Change artifacts.

In particular, recommendations concerning test scenarios should initially remain research findings rather than silently changing the validation framework.

## Expected Outcome

The desired outcome is not a declaration that Threadwright is unique.

The desired outcome is a clearer answer to:

> **Where does Threadwright sit within the existing intellectual and practical landscape of human–agent collaboration, and which parts of Threadwright are genuinely necessary once that landscape is understood?**

The investigation should make Threadwright **less isolated, less redundant, better grounded, and more precise**.

Only after this positioning work should claims about Threadwright's distinctive contribution be strengthened.
