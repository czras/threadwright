# RESEARCH-0004: Agentic Product Management and Software Engineering Toolset

**Status:** Deferred

## Research Question

What can Threadwright learn from the emerging ecosystem of agentic product-management and software-engineering tools and methodologies?

## Motivation

Threadwright is developing as a methodology for human-agent collaborative product and software engineering. An emerging ecosystem of tools, skills, workflows, and methodologies is addressing overlapping problems from different perspectives.

Rather than independently reinventing capabilities that already exist, Threadwright should understand this ecosystem and identify ideas that may strengthen its own practice.

The purpose of this research is not to determine which tool is "best". It is to understand the underlying approaches, assumptions, capabilities, and trade-offs, and determine what Threadwright should adopt, adapt, reject, or leave deliberately outside its methodology.

## Initial Scope

The initial exploration includes:

- Get Shit Done (GSD)
- Matt Pocock's skills
- BMad Method / BMaD skills
- Nautilus
- SpecKit
- OpenDesign

The scope may expand if the investigation reveals other relevant approaches.

These initiatives should not be assumed to be directly comparable. They may operate at different layers, address different problems, or represent different kinds of artifacts, skills, methodologies, or tooling. Establishing these distinctions is itself part of the research.

## Areas of Investigation

The research may examine:

1. **Problem and intent**
   - What problem is the approach trying to solve?
   - What does it consider the practitioner's actual need?

2. **Practitioner–agent relationship**
   - What role does the human play?
   - What role does the agent play?
   - Where does judgment reside?
   - How are authority and responsibility handled?

3. **Workflow and control**
   - What pursuit or development loop does the approach establish?
   - How is progress determined?
   - How are consequential gaps handled?
   - Where and how does human intervention occur?

4. **Artifacts and state**
   - What persistent artifacts are created?
   - What information is considered durable state?
   - How does the approach maintain continuity across activities?

5. **Context and cognition**
   - How is context supplied to agents?
   - How is intermediate or unfinished cognition handled?
   - What information is deliberately preserved or discarded?

6. **Capabilities**
   - What capabilities does the approach provide?
   - Which are methodological?
   - Which are implementation or tooling choices?
   - Which capabilities might be useful to Threadwright?

7. **Assumptions and trade-offs**
   - What assumptions does each approach make?
   - What constraints does it introduce?
   - What failure modes or limitations become apparent?

8. **Relationship to Threadwright**
   - What should Threadwright adopt?
   - What should it adapt?
   - What should it reject?
   - What should it deliberately leave outside the methodology?

## Important Research Constraint

The investigation should avoid premature feature-by-feature comparison.

The objective is first to understand each approach on its own terms and identify the underlying ideas it embodies. Comparison should follow from that understanding rather than imposing Threadwright's current conceptual model onto the investigated approaches.

## Expected Outcome

The research should produce a set of findings about the agentic product-management and software-engineering ecosystem and their relevance to Threadwright.

Potential outcomes include:

- methodological ideas worth incorporating into Threadwright,
- capabilities that should be implemented or delegated to existing tooling,
- assumptions Threadwright should explicitly reject,
- distinctions between methodology and implementation that clarify Threadwright's boundaries,
- gaps in the current Threadwright model,
- or confirmation that existing Threadwright concepts are independently reflected elsewhere.

No particular outcome is assumed in advance.

## Relationships

This research may intersect with research into practitioner judgment and human-agent collaboration, particularly where investigated approaches make assumptions about human authority, agent autonomy, or the distribution of cognitive work.

It may also intersect with Threadwright observations concerning intermediate cognition, persistent artifacts, and the relationship between activities and durable state.

## Deferral Rationale

The ecosystem is relevant to Threadwright, but immediate comprehensive investigation is not currently required.

Threadwright is still being developed through direct practice and observation. Existing approaches should be investigated when understanding them is consequential to a Threadwright decision, capability, or design direction.

Until then, this research direction is preserved for future pursuit.
